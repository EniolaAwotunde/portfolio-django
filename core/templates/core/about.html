About Me

Hi, I'm Eniola Roseline Awotunde, a software developer passionate about building practical, user-focused digital products.

I enjoy solving problems, learning new technologies and turning ideas into functional applications. I am particularly interested in software engineering, full-stack and backend development, and I am motivated by opportunities to build meaningful products and solve real-world problems through technology.

I am open to junior, entry-level and internship opportunities in software development, and I am eager to contribute to a team while continuing to learn, grow and take on new technical challenges.
